**Tools:** Python and pandas (cleaned data, 20 key questions answered)

---

## 6. Attrition (Employees Who Left)

**392 employees left, an overall attrition rate of 39.2%.**

### Attrition by Job Satisfaction

| Satisfaction Score | Total Employees | Employees Left | Attrition Rate |
| ------------------ | --------------- | -------------- | -------------- |
| 1                  | 218             | 128            | 58.72%         |
| 2                  | 214             | 114            | 53.27%         |
| 3                  | 182             | 50             | 27.47%         |
| 4                  | 206             | 54             | 26.21%         |
| 5                  | 180             | 46             | 25.56%         |

**Findings**

- Employees with a satisfaction score of **1 or 2** left at 53% to 59%.
- Employees with a score of **3, 4 or 5** left at about 26% to 27%.
- Employees at scores 1 and 2 account for 242 of the 392 who left (about 62%).
- The rate barely changes between scores 3, 4 and 5. The big drop happens between 2 and 3.
- This shows a link between low satisfaction and leaving. It does not prove that low satisfaction caused people to leave.

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **Satisfaction is the clearest warning sign for attrition.** Anyone scoring 1 or 2 is much more likely to leave.
2. **Finance needs a closer look.** It has the lowest satisfaction (2.72) and the most employees (218).
3. **Pay does not reflect performance.** Salary is almost flat across performance scores.
4. **Pay is fairly even** across departments and between men and women.
5. **Overall attrition of 39.2% is high** and worth raising with the business.

---

## Things to Keep in Mind

- **Age** is worked out from Birth Year using 2024 as the reference year.
- **Benefits (42.9%)** counts all 1,000 employees, including the 210 blanks. Among only the 790 who answered, the share enrolled is about 54.3%.
- **Years of service** comes from the original column, which contradicts the joining dates. These figures will change once a new tenure column is built from the cleaned joining dates.
- **Sick days, vacation days and training hours** are blank for most employees (222, 399 and 327 have values), so those averages only reflect part of the workforce.
